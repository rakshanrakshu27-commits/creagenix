Study Guide: The Power of Active Recall

Active recall is a principle of efficient learning, which claims the need to actively stimulate memory retrieval during the learning process. It contrasts with passive review, where learning material is processed passively (e.g., by reading or watching).

Testing yourself through practice questions, such as the MCQs provided on CreaGenix, forces your brain to retrieve information, creating stronger neural connections and improving long-term memory retention.

Study Guide: The Feynman Technique

The Feynman Technique is a four-step process for understanding any concept deeply:

  • Choose a concept: Pick a topic you want to learn.
  • Teach it to a child: Explain it in simple terms as if to someone with no background knowledge.
  • Identify gaps: Locate where you struggle in your explanation and revisit source material.
  • Review and simplify: Refine your explanation until simple and clear.
