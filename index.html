Memory Science

The Zeigarnik Effect

Your brain naturally tracks uncompleted tasks better than completed ones. Taking planned breaks mid-problem makes you more likely to remember the concept long-term!

Biology Focus

Blue Light vs Retinal Focus

Looking at blue light screens right before studying drops reading comprehension speed by up to 22%. Switch to a physical book or use eye comfort warm-mode filters.

Cognitive Hack

The Feynman Secret Formula

If you cannot explain a highly complicated concept to a 10-year-old child using basic everyday terms, you do not fully grasp that concept yourself yet.

Neuroscience

Scent-Induced Memory Triggers

Chewing a specific rare flavor of chewing gum while studying, and then chewing that exact same flavor during your test, triggers instant contextual memory recall inside the brain.

Study Optimization

The Curve of Forgetting

Without review, your brain dumps up to 80% of newly learned information within 48 hours. Reviewing your content for just 5 minutes daily resets this memory decay loop back to 100%.
